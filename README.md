# Market Entry Strategy for a Direct-to-Consumer Skincare Brand in Tier-2 India

**Context & Affiliation**: Management Consulting Case, IIT Roorkee  
**Domain**: Management Consulting, Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy, Financial Modeling, Consumer Retail  
**Focus Brand**: GlowCare (D2C Premium Skincare Expansion into Tier-2 Indian Metros).

---

## 1. Problem Statement
While tier-1 Indian metros have reached digital skincare market saturation, tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Indore, Lucknow, Coimbatore) exhibit rapid disposable income growth and high online beauty adoption. This project evaluates the strategic viability and financial feasibility of expanding GlowCare into tier-2 India, structuring a phased market entry plan and unit economics model.

---

## 2. Consulting Methodology & Analytical Pipeline
- **Market Sizing Funnel (TAM/SAM/SOM)**: Quantified the tier-2 personal care addressable market using top-down census demographics and bottom-up e-commerce transaction data, sizing a ₹450 Cr serviceable addressable market.
- **Customer Segmentation**: Segmented consumers into three distinct personas (*Young Professionals*, *College Students*, *Homemakers*) based on price sensitivity, social media discovery channels, and ingredient preferences.
- **Dynamic 3-Year Financial Model**: Developed a complete financial P&L model in Python (`05_financial_model.py`), simulating revenues, gross margins, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and working capital.
- **Phased GTM Execution**: Structured a 2-phase rollout (Phase 1: Jaipur, Indore, Lucknow, Coimbatore; Phase 2: Surat, Nagpur, Bhopal, Kochi), pairing D2C website sales with regional influencer marketing and quick-commerce channels.

---

## 3. Financial & Strategic Outcomes
- **₹450 Cr Addressable Market**: Sized untapped whitespace in regional premium skincare demand.
- **14-Month Break-Even Horizon**: Modeled positive unit contribution margins with a target LTV-to-CAC ratio exceeding 3.2:1.
- **15% Projected Margin Improvement**: Achieved through direct channel fulfillment optimization and tiered SKU bundling.
